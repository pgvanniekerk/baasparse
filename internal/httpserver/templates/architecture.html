Architecture & roadmap

What this alpha is — and the telco mediation engine it grows into.

A high-performance, memory-efficient, configurable mediation layer

baasparse sits between the network elements that produce usage records and the systems that consume them — billing, revenue assurance, fraud, analytics. It collects, decodes, validates, deduplicates, correlates, enriches, transforms and distributes usage records losslessly, and auditably.

Zero record lossevery record delivered, discarded, or suspended — always accounted for
No unintended duplicationidempotent recovery, deterministic output identity
Provable completenessinput conservation: in = out + suspended + discarded
